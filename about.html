About Me

Welcome to my personal website.

I am an Assistant Professor (Commerce) (HES-II) at Department of Higher Education, Government of Haryana specializing in Accounting and Finance. I have been teaching and researching in this field for over 9 years.

Areas of interest include accounting, finance, sustainability, and broader economic issues. Skilled in advanced Excel, data analysis, and AI-assisted research methodologies, these tools are actively embedded into teaching practices and academic writing. Student mentoring spans research projects, skill enhancement and preparation for competitive examinations.

Driven by a commitment to interdisciplinary inquiry and technology-enabled pedagogy, with an ongoing interest in fostering critical thinking and innovation within higher education. Open to collaborative initiatives exploring the research intersections.
